## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere ("The Sacred Sanctuary")
A fusion of Scandinavian minimalism and classical Islamic architectural aesthetics:
- **Canvas:** Deep Obsidian Midnight Forest (`#0B1512`)
- **Primary Brand:** Deep Emerald Green (`#0F4C3A`)
- **Radiant Accents:** Warm Metallic Gold (`#D4AF37`) & Mint Glow (`#10B981`)
- **Surfaces:** Frosted Glass (`#13221C`) with 16px backdrop-blur and ultra-subtle translucent borders (0.12 opacity)
- **Typography:** Manrope (headlines) + Inter (body) + Classical Arabic (Amiri / Scheherazade New)
